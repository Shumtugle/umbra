# Plans

## The stories

Every realm has a story, opened with the question mark in the top right corner
of the screen. The stories are written separately; until then each realm shows
a short placeholder. A story lives in `assets/stories/<realm key>.txt`, plain
text, paragraphs separated by an empty line, read in a serif face on a dark
page.

Each story is an essay of the same five parts:

1. **The experiment.** What was done or imagined, and how, told so that it can
   be pictured.
2. **The history.** When and where, who was there, what was believed before,
   what changed after.
3. **The idea.** The philosophical concept under it: what it says about the
   world, about knowing, about us.
4. **The people.** Names, with a line on each.
5. **In the game.** How the realm turns the idea into a law of the ball, and
   which sign on the screen shows it.

### Written realms

| key | realm | the experiment | the people | the idea |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `hole` | The hole | dark stars that light cannot leave; a solution of the field equations with a horizon; the first image of a shadow | Michell, Laplace, Schwarzschild, Chandrasekhar, Penrose, Hawking | a place from which nothing returns; the edge of what can be known |

| `lyceum` | The Lyceum, 335 BC | the thrown stone in the books on nature: violent motion, natural place; later, the theory of impetus | Aristotle, Philoponus, Buridan; Eudoxus and Ptolemy for the cosmos in the mosaic | why a wrong theory lasts two thousand years; common sense as a theory |
| `river` | The Black River, 1837 | a winter duel by a frozen river; the wind as a force on a flying body | the poet and his opponent, the seconds; Beaufort for the wind | honour, chance and fate; what a rule is when it is a ritual |

## The game

Done so far, checked on the stand and, where marked, on the phone:

- Eight realms, played in a shuffled order after the hole (on the phone: they
  look right).
- The cabinet of curiosities, the story pages, matches that wait.
- Realms in versions, from a bag of their own: the darkroom's thirty-one eyes,
  each with a card of its own before the realm's essay.
- Larger ball, story button and lettering (on the phone: easier to use, the
  realm's name can be read).

Next, in this order:

1. The wormhole, then the collider with the rally drawn as a diagram, then
   tunnelling, the interferometer, time dilation, the pulsar, dark matter.
2. The carousel: the table rocks under a still camera, the ball flies straight;
   a pair to the station. Tachyons: a soft stroke flies fastest, the tail runs
   ahead of the ball.
3. The round table: a ring of opponents on the far side turns after every
   stroke, each with his own arms and habit; a knight who loses a point leaves
   and his shield is turned over; the perilous seat answers by itself, and in
   the deeper rounds a knight in white takes it. Leonardo's notebook: every
   stroke leaves a vortex that lives a few seconds and drifts the next ball;
   a sepia page, mirror writing, his mechanical knight among the opponents.
4. The board: each match sets a position from a famous game, about a hundred
   of them, every one checked by replaying the game's moves. Pieces stop the
   ball, tip over and fall off the table; the king rocks and stays standing.
   The material knocked down is counted, and the cabinet keeps the best piece.
5. The big top: the camera climbs under the dome and looks almost straight
   down; the table is a trampoline whose dent rocks and fades; a ball that
   lands while the cloth rises gains height, one that lands while it sinks
   loses it; a fresh dent tilts the next bounce sideways; a tightrope walker
   crosses along the net. The board and the round table come in versions
   the way the darkroom does: one position, or one ring of knights, per match.
6. Galileo's ship, Zeno, epicycles, harmony, the written future, mixed in
   among them.
7. The stories, one essay per realm, in five parts as above.
8. Phone check: the paddle's lift above the finger, the strike ring, sound
   latency, frame rate on the phone and on the tablet, the swipe that fires a
   shot.
9. Difficulty measured with a living hand, not only the stand's bot; longer
   rallies on the first rounds.
10. A second black hole in the deeper rounds of the hole.
11. Paddles that carry the law of their realm wherever it fits.
12. A live project page in `docs/`: the game in the browser, played with a
    finger.
13. Releases and the project page are made by the author; the line on the
    author's showcase goes through the duty desk.
