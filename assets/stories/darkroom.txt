The darkroom. On this side of the net the world is as it is; on that side it is an image, and every match a different eye makes it. The full story of how people learned to see what the eye alone cannot, and of the painters who learned to see the seeing, is still being written: the camera obscura and the first fixed image, the negative, the invisible light past both ends of the rainbow, the books on colour the painters read, and the old cave, where all anyone ever saw were shadows on a wall.
