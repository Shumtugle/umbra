A courtyard in Athens. Here a struck ball is given an impetus, a push of its own: it flies in a straight line while the impetus lasts, and then, spent, it falls straight down to its natural place.

A line runs from the ball to where its impetus will end, a plumb line drops from there, and a ring marks the place on the table. Under the table lies the cosmos in mosaic: the earth at the centre, the elements round it, the spheres of the planets, the fixed stars.

The full story of this realm, its history and its ideas, is still being written.
