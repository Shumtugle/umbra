A station turns in orbit, and the table stands on its glass deck. Nothing floats over the net: the turning room itself carries every ball aside, the same way from either end.

Under the glass the stars and the planet wheel round at the very rate of the spin, and the ball turns the way the sky turns. The grid on the table is twisted the same way, and lanes of light trace the curve of a ball in each direction.

The full story of this realm, its history and its ideas, is still being written.
