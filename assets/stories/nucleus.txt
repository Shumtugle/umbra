A laboratory early in the twentieth century. Over the net hangs an atomic nucleus, and it pushes the ball away, harder the closer it passes. A ball that comes straight at it may be thrown back the way it came.

The grid on the table is pressed into a knot under the nucleus, chalk arrows drift outward round it, the ball leaves a trail of mist, and the blackboard holds the old drawing of particles scattered by a nucleus.

The full story of this realm, its history and its ideas, is still being written.
