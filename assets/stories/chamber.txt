A cloud chamber: a glass vessel of vapour on the edge of turning to mist, a strong lamp from the side, black velvet below, and two coils that fill its middle with a magnetic field rising out of the table.

Every charged thing that crosses the vapour leaves a trail of droplets, and in the field every trail curls. Each struck ball carries a charge, red for positive, blue for negative. In the field a red ball turns one way and a blue one the other; outside it, the ball flies straight. Stray particles from the sky cross the chamber all the while: electrons in tight spirals, now and then a positron spiralling the other way.

The full story of this realm, its history and its ideas, is still being written.
