A winter evening by a frozen river, and a duel fought with paddles, where nobody falls.

The wind carries the ball sideways, and every few seconds a gust crosses the table as a travelling wave. The snow shows it before it arrives. A paddle that comes forward to its barrier strikes harder. Each side has one shot a match: the hardest ball there is, with a crack and a cloud of smoke that the wind carries away.

The full story of this realm, its history and its ideas, is still being written.
