In place of the net stands a screen of smoked glass with two slits in it. A ball must go through one of them; a ball that meets the glass is stopped.

Behind the screen, light and dark fringes lie across the table, and the ball cannot come down on a dark one: past the slits it is drawn sideways to the nearest bright fringe, as if it had gone through both slits at once and met itself. A lamp on the screen watches the left slit. When it is lit, it is known which slit the ball went through, the fringes fade, and the ball flies like any plain ball.

Every landing leaves a dot on the table, and over a match the dots build up the fringes, one ball at a time. In the deeper rounds a shadow ball from a world next to this one flies through the other slit beside the real one.

The full story of this realm, its history and its ideas, is still being written.
